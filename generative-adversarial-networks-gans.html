The Mathematics of Generative Adversarial Networks

Generative vs Discriminative Models

While discriminative models map inputs to labels (P(Y|X)), generative models learn the joint probability distribution (P(X,Y)) or P(X) directly, allowing them to synthesize new data.

The Minimax Game and Nash Equilibrium

GANs consist of a Generator (G) and Discriminator (D) locked in a minimax game: min_G max_D V(D,G) = E[log(D(x))] + E[log(1 - D(G(z)))]. Training seeks a Nash Equilibrium where G perfectly fools D.

Mode Collapse and Solutions

Mode collapse occurs when G produces limited varieties of outputs. Solutions include Wasserstein GAN (WGAN) using Earth Mover's Distance, spectral normalization to enforce Lipschitz continuity, and progressive growing of layers.


import torch.nn as nn
class Generator(nn.Module):
    def __init__(self):
        super(Generator, self).__init__()
        self.main = nn.Sequential(
            nn.Linear(100, 256),
            nn.ReLU(True),
            nn.Linear(256, 512),
            nn.ReLU(True),
            nn.Linear(512, 1024),
            nn.Tanh()
        )
    def forward(self, x):
        return self.main(x)
